## Provenance

Each selected recipe is traceable via its `source_id` (Wikibooks filename) and its original source URL (Wikibooks Cookbook page). Provenance is preserved in the canonical working dataset.

## Data Handling

* **Fidelity**: Raw source data (ingredients and instructions) was extracted without alteration of meaning.
* **Qualitative Preservation**: Qualitative measurements (e.g., "to taste", "pinch") are preserved as strings.
* **No Fabrication**: No numeric values or units were invented during normalization.
* **Separation**: Recipe quantities remain distinct from pantry availability logic.
