SOURCE C — MIXED OPEN / PUBLIC-DOMAIN SOURCES
100 recipes

For the third 100, I'm deliberately diversifying the corpus instead of taking another 100 from the same book.

The major sources here are:

Project Gutenberg
Internet Archive public-domain scans
Wikimedia Commons public-domain cookbook scans

Project Gutenberg's The A B C of Cooking is explicitly a public-domain cookbook and provides a particularly useful set of straightforward American camp/home recipes.

The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book is another large public-domain-era source available through Project Gutenberg, with chapters covering beef, pork, poultry, vegetables, salads, entrées, desserts, pastry, sandwiches, etc.

Wikimedia Commons also hosts public-domain cookbook scans from the Internet Archive, including The White House Cook Book, The Complete Cook Book, The Standard Book of Recipes, The Original Buckeye Cook Book, The Rural Cook Book, and others.
