# Use-It-Up

Your companion for mindful cooking. Discover recipes based on what you already have in your kitchen.

## Features
- **The Home Feed**: Personalized Top 5 recommendations based on your current pantry.
- **The Kitchen Hub**: Manage your pantry, track leftovers, and keep a consolidated grocery list.
- **Interactive Recipes**: See exactly what you have and what's missing. Add gaps to your grocery list with a single tap.
- **Earthy Kitchen Design**: A high-contrast, cozy UI designed for the kitchen environment.
- **Local-First**: All your data stays on your device.

## Tech Stack
- Native Android (Kotlin)
- Jetpack Compose
- Room Persistence
- Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)
- Navigation 3
